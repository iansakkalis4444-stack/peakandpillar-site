How much do inspections cost?

Essentials Walk-Through is $395. Standard Facility Check is $795 (larger sites quoted). The Quarterly Care Plan is $295 per visit, or $495 for a once-a-year check. An extra unit on the same site is $150. Travel outside our usual southern Ontario and GTA area is quoted. Vendor and project management is quoted per project. All prices are in CAD, plus HST. The first 5 founding clients get the Essentials Walk-Through for $195.

What does a facility inspection cover?

A visual, non-invasive walk-through of accessible areas. That includes roof and exterior, HVAC (visual), plumbing leaks, visible electrical hazards (no covers opened), fire safety equipment status, slip and trip hazards, parking and lighting, accessibility basics, housekeeping and storage, signs of pests, and ranked maintenance priorities.

What does an inspection not cover?

It is not a structural or building-systems assessment by a licensed professional, an ASTM E2018 property condition assessment, a code or fire inspection, a designated substance survey, an environmental assessment or an AODA audit. There is no testing, no code or fire compliance sign-off, and no repair cost estimates.

How long until I get my report?

Essentials Walk-Through: PDF report in 3 business days. Standard Facility Check: report in 5 business days. For Care Plan visits and other work, we confirm timing when we book.

Where do you work?

We serve southern Ontario and the Greater Toronto Area. Peak & Pillar is based in Oakville, Ontario, with no storefront — every inspection happens on site. Travel outside our usual area is quoted.

Who is this for?

Small property managers and small business owners who want a clear, photo-backed picture of their building and a ranked list of what to deal with first.

Do you manage contractors after an inspection?

Yes. Vendor and project management is an add-on, quoted per project. We bring in trusted contractors, gather quotes, schedule the work and oversee it to completion.

Scope

What the walk-through covers

A visual, non-invasive check of accessible areas, including:

  • Roof and exterior
  • HVAC (visual)
  • Plumbing leaks
  • Visible electrical hazards (no covers opened)
  • Fire safety equipment status: extinguisher tags, exit and emergency lights, propped fire doors, blocked sprinklers
  • Slip and trip hazards
  • Parking and lighting
  • Accessibility basics
  • Housekeeping and storage
  • Signs of pests
  • Ranked maintenance priorities (no repair cost estimates)

Please note

Every inspection is a visual, non-invasive walk-through of accessible areas. It is not a structural or building-systems assessment by a licensed professional, an ASTM E2018 property condition assessment, a code or fire inspection, a designated substance survey, an environmental assessment or an AODA audit. No compliance certification and no repair cost estimates are provided. Liability is limited to the fee paid.

Contractors are hired and paid directly by the client. Quotes come from the contractors, and Peak & Pillar isn't responsible for their workmanship.
